OUR STORY

From our kitchen
to your table.

Philo Cooks is a family-owned Nigerian food business built around something simple: good food, made the way we love to make it at home.

Every meal is prepared with authentic Nigerian recipes, fresh ingredients, and the care that comes from cooking for family. We prepare meals based on customer orders, so you can reach out and ask about the dishes, portions, and availability you want.
